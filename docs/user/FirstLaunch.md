# First launch

## The setup assistant

On its first start Kaichii opens a three-step assistant. You can open it again at any time from
*Help > Setup Assistant*.

1. **Your games.** Add the folders where your games are. Kaichii lists the games in them and looks
   again each time it starts. Switch 2 games packed as NUP files need nothing else; NSP and XCI files
   also need the decryption keys of your console, which you can install from here (see
   [Games, keys and firmware](Games.md)).
2. **This computer.** Kaichii shows the graphics card, video memory, processor and memory it found,
   and recommends one of four presets. Keep the recommendation unless you know better; you can
   change it later in *Configure > Performance* (see [Settings for your computer](Performance.md)).
3. **Console mode and controllers.** *Docked* draws more detail at a higher resolution, as on a TV;
   *Handheld* is lighter and suits slower computers. Player 1 starts on the keyboard; *Configure
   controls* picks a controller for it.

## The game list

Double-click a game to play. Right-click it for more:

- **Configure Game**: settings for that game only. Its first tab, *Summary*, lists what the game
  changes from your global settings, with a button to go back to the global value.
- **Optimize for This Game**: Kaichii measures the game as you play and proposes settings (see
  [Settings for your computer](Performance.md#optimize-for-this-game)).
- **Submit compatibility report**: after playing, tell how it went (see
  [Sending a compatibility report](CompatibilityReports.md)).

With no games yet, the list offers to add a games folder or to open the assistant.

## Settings

*Emulation > Configure* opens the settings, grouped by what they are for: Performance, Image,
Smoothness and shaders, Audio, Controls, System and Interface.

- **Every option explains itself.** Point at an option, or reach it with the keyboard, and the panel
  at the bottom says what it does, what it costs and what is recommended for this computer.
- **The basic view** shows what most players need. *Show advanced options* adds the rest, and
  *Developer mode* the options for debugging.
- **The search box** finds any setting by its name or description.

## The status bar

The buttons at the bottom of the main window show the graphics API, the GPU accuracy, the console
mode, the window filter, the anti-aliasing and more. Point at one for what it means; click it to
change it.
