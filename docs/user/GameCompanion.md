# Game Companion

The Game Companion is an optional, offline reference panel for two games:

- **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on Switch 2** (program ID `0400486024DEC000`).
- **The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild on Switch** (program ID `01007EF00011E000`); see
  [Breath of the Wild](#breath-of-the-wild) below.

Open it from **View > Show Game Companion** while one of them is selected or running, or
right-click the game in the library and choose **Open Game Companion**. Each game has its own
cards, topics and colors, and the panel switches when you change game. It is off by default. Drag its title bar to move or float it; close it
with its × button or the View menu.

Search for places, characters, enemies, bosses, dungeons, items, songs, remake changes and the
series timeline, or filter by category. Cards offer context, interesting details and, where useful,
practical tips and a source link. The bestiary covers the 77 distinct names recorded across the
Hall of Fallen Foes' 84 statue entries, plus Gohma Eggs. Some statues represent variants of the
same foe. A card opens only when you pick a topic; the **✕** in its top right corner closes it
and gives the space back to the list. The panel does **not** read the game's
current coordinates or recognise enemies on screen.

While playing from a `.nup` file, the panel can suggest a card for a known area or boss arena
when the game loads that scene's parameters or baked graphics resources. This also
detects returns to areas whose parameters are already cached. Choose **Open card** to view it:
the button confirms
the action and the suggestion disappears. The suggestion does not replace the card you are reading
or change your filters by itself. It reflects the **last loaded scene**,
not an exact position. Other game formats and scenes without a matching card still use manual
search. The Companion does not contact an AI provider, send screenshots or fetch wiki pages on
its own; clicking a source link opens that page in your browser.

**Show story spoilers** is off by default. With it off, later bosses, dungeons and plot reveals
are absent from search. The explanation of the timeline branches is hidden too. Other cards show
their details without those reveals. Spoilers switch off again when the panel closes. The catalog
is a reference guide, not a complete walkthrough.
Scene suggestions also respect this setting: a boss or area marked as a spoiler stays hidden
until you turn it on.

The cards are written for Kaichii. Zelda Dungeon's [Hall of Fallen Foes
transcription](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Ocarina_of_Time_Hall_of_Fallen_Foes_Statues)
records names, locations and useful items from the Switch 2 edition. The remake indexes for
[places](https://zelda.mtwildwood.net/en-gb/places/),
[dungeons](https://zelda.mtwildwood.net/en-gb/dungeons/),
[characters](https://zelda.mtwildwood.net/en-gb/characters/) and
[items](https://zelda.mtwildwood.net/en-gb/items/) organize the other topics. Nintendo's
[official 40th anniversary timeline](https://www.nintendo.com/es-es/Campanias-publicitarias/40-aniversario-de-The-Legend-of-Zelda/40-aniversario-de-The-Legend-of-Zelda-Cronologia-3214722.html)
places Ocarina of Time before the three-way split. The [original game's
wiki](https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time) provides historical
context, and the [remake page](https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time_(Switch_2))
records some edition-specific changes. Each card links to its source. Tactics from the original
game are presented as guidance where they have not yet been checked in the remake.

## Breath of the Wild

The Breath of the Wild cards cover regions and settlements, characters, enemies, bosses, the
Divine Beasts and shrines, equipment, the Sheikah Slate's runes and the Champions' abilities,
the Expansion Pass, the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, and the story of the Great Calamity. Every card
is written for Kaichii and links to the [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Breath_of_the_Wild)
article it was checked against. Spanish titles use the Spanish edition's names where they are well
known.

Scene suggestions work with `.nsp` and `.xci` files, with or without the update. Entering a shrine
suggests the *Shrines of Trials* card (the game does not say which shrine by itself), and entering
a Divine Beast suggests that beast's card. The suggestion goes away when Link is back in the open
world. Regions are not suggested: the open world streams around Link wherever he goes, so a
loaded map section is not a reliable place name. With spoilers off, King Rhoam, Dark Beast Ganon,
the Blights and the expansion's final boss stay hidden.
